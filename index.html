الفجوة المعرفية
The Problem

Most online Islamic learning is fragmented, shallow, and unrooted.

No sequence.

Self-directed study skips prerequisites. Topics arrive out of order. Gaps form quietly and compound.

No depth.

Tutorials and short videos optimize for engagement, not mastery. Material is summarized rather than studied.

No chain.

Knowledge passed without verification loses its weight. The traditional sciences require teachers, not just texts.

الأسئلة الشائعة
Common Questions

Frequently asked.

Who is eligible to apply?
Applications are open to adult learners committed to structured, sequential study. There are no geographic restrictions. Each applicant is assessed individually on academic background and stated intentions.
Is prior knowledge of Arabic required?
Not for the Arabic Language program, which begins at the foundational level. Students applying to Fiqh, Hadith, or other programs that depend on Arabic comprehension are expected to have a working knowledge of the language, or to enroll in the Arabic program first.
How are classes conducted?
Classes are delivered online through scheduled live sessions, with assigned readings and assessments between sessions. Recordings are provided for review, but live attendance is expected.
What is the time commitment?
Students should expect a minimum of five to seven hours per week per course, including class time, reading, and review. This is structured study, not passive consumption.
What happens after I apply?
Completed applications are reviewed by the admissions team. Applicants will be contacted within two to three weeks with a decision or a request for additional information. Not all applicants are accepted.
